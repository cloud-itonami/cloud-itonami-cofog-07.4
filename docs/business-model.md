# Business Model: Independent Community Vector-Control & Environmental Health Monitoring

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-cofog-07.4`
- COFOG: `07.4` (Public health services)
- Activity: vector-control field survey and environmental-health
  surveillance reporting
- Social impact: earlier outbreak-risk detection, healthier communities,
  transparent public-health surveillance

## Customer

- municipal / county public-health departments
- large campuses and event venues requiring vector-control compliance
- disaster-response agencies needing rapid environmental-health surveys

## Offer

- scheduled + on-demand vector/environmental-health field survey
- risk-scored surveillance report with sensor/imaging evidence
- routine remediation recommendation (standing-water removal, trap
  servicing)
- outbreak-risk escalation straight to the public-health authority (human
  sign-off, never automated)
- surveillance-history audit export for public-health reporting

## Revenue

- per-zone survey fee
- municipal/county subscription (recurring surveillance contract)
- event/campus compliance package
- compliance/audit export package

## Trust Controls

- risk-scoring recommendations require Public Health Governor clearance
- any `:high`/`:safety-critical` outbreak-risk finding always escalates to
  human sign-off and the public-health authority
- every survey, finding and escalation is logged
- public health-risk claims must reference measured sensor/surveillance
  data
